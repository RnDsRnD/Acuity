Acuity Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Acuity (“Acuity,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the Acuity PANCE/PANRE exam-prep app.

Acuity is designed as an offline-first study app. The app provides board-style practice questions, explanations, spaced repetition, exam simulation, bookmarking, and performance tracking for educational purposes.

This policy is provided for transparency and is not a substitute for legal advice.

2. Information Acuity stores locally on your device

Acuity stores study progress locally on your device using local app storage. This may include:

  • Questions answered
  • Selected answers
  • Correct/incorrect history
  • Question bookmarks
  • Missed-question status
  • Category performance
  • Quick Study spaced-repetition due dates
  • Daily activity/streak information
  • Onboarding completion status

This information is used only to provide app features such as progress tracking, performance statistics, spaced repetition, missed-question review, bookmarked-question review, and exam-prep analytics.

Acuity does not treat this information as medical data, and it is not intended to store personal health information.

3. Information we collect directly

At this time, Acuity does not require users to create an account, log in, or submit personal information directly to us.

Acuity does not intentionally collect:

  • Name
  • Address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Personal health records
  • Government identifiers

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Acuity may offer optional paid access through subscriptions or lifetime purchase options.

Purchases are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. When you make or restore a purchase, Google Play and RevenueCat may process information necessary to complete, validate, and manage that purchase, such as:

  • Purchase status
  • Product identifier
  • Transaction information
  • Subscription status
  • Device/app identifiers needed for purchase validation
  • RevenueCat customer identifier

We use this information to determine whether you have access to Acuity Pro features.

We do not receive your full payment card information. Payment processing is handled by Google Play and its payment systems.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Third-party services

Acuity may use the following third-party services:

Google Play

Google Play is used for Android app distribution and payment processing.

RevenueCat

RevenueCat is used to manage subscriptions, lifetime purchases, purchase restoration, and Pro entitlement status.

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control their independent privacy practices.

6. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Load and display study questions
  • Track progress and performance
  • Show missed, bookmarked, unanswered, and weak-category study modes
  • Manage Quick Study spaced repetition
  • Verify Pro access
  • Restore purchases
  • Improve the reliability of the app experience

7. Data sharing

Acuity does not sell your personal data.

Acuity does not share your locally stored study progress with us or with third parties unless a future version adds a feature that explicitly requires syncing or account-based storage.

Purchase-related information may be processed by Google Play and RevenueCat to provide and verify paid access.

8. Data retention

Study progress stored locally remains on your device until:

  • You clear the app’s data
  • You uninstall the app
  • The app provides a reset option and you use it
  • Your device operating system removes the data

Purchase and subscription records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their own policies and legal obligations.

9. Data deletion

Because Acuity currently stores study progress locally on your device and does not require an account, you can delete local app data by:

  • Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or
  • Uninstalling the app

For purchase-related data handled by Google Play or RevenueCat, deletion and retention may depend on those services’ policies and legal requirements.

You may contact us at daltdris22@gmail.com with privacy-related questions or requests.

10. Children’s privacy

Acuity is intended for physician assistant students, clinicians, and adult learners preparing for PANCE/PANRE-style exams. It is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Educational and medical disclaimer

Acuity is an educational exam-prep tool. It is not medical advice, clinical decision support, diagnosis, or treatment guidance.

Users should rely on qualified clinical training, current professional guidelines, instructors, and licensed healthcare professionals for real patient care decisions.

12. Security

Acuity uses local device storage for study progress and uses established third-party services for purchase handling. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but the app is designed to limit the amount of personal information handled directly by the developer.

13. International users

Acuity may be available to users in different countries. If you use the app outside the United States, you understand that app distribution, payment processing, and subscription management may involve systems operated by Google Play, RevenueCat, or other service providers.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Continued use of Acuity after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
